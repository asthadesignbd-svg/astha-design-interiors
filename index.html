About ASTHA

Thoughtful spaces. Timeless design.

At ASTHA Design & Interiors, we believe good design should look beautiful and work beautifully. We plan spaces with care, balancing comfort, function, materials and detail around the people who use them.

From homes to offices and commercial spaces, our aim is to turn your ideas into interiors that feel personal, practical and lasting.
